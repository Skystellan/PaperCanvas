# PaperCanvas v0.2.0 — Your papers. Your network.

**拖动论文，连接思路，构建属于自己的论文网络。**

The first public release of PaperCanvas: a local-first desktop workspace for
turning a paper library into a visual research network.

![PaperCanvas demo](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Skystellan/PaperCanvas/v0.2.0/docs/media/paper-network-demo.gif)

## Highlights / 核心体验

- **Drag papers onto your canvas.** 从论文库拖入画布，自由组织研究主题。
- **Connect your ideas.** Create relationships, mark support or challenges, and attach explanations and evidence.
- **Shape your network.** Smooth dragging, backgrounds that follow each topic's cards, and neighboring groups that move aside together. Connections gradually separate after dragging settles.
- **Read and think in one place.** PDF search, highlights, comments, local Markdown notes, Markmap mind maps, and optional embedded ChatGPT discussions.
- **Keep your work local.** Papers, layouts, annotations, and notes stay on your Mac. ChatGPT is an optional online service; papers are not sent automatically.

## Download and install / 下载安装

**Apple Silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 13+.** This release does not include Intel Mac, Windows, or Linux binaries.

1. Download **`PaperCanvas-0.2.0-macOS-arm64.zip`** below.
2. Unzip and move **PaperCanvas.app** to **Applications**.
3. Open the app and import your PDFs. If upgrading, quit the old app before replacing it; your existing library, notes, and settings remain in their separate data directory.

This build is **ad-hoc signed**, without an Apple Developer ID or notarization.
If macOS blocks the first launch and you trust the download, open **System Settings →
Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** after attempting to open the app.
[Apple's instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445)

首次启动如被 macOS 拦截，请在确认下载来源后，前往「系统设置 → 隐私与安全 → 仍要打开」。
本版本尚未经过 Apple 公证。

## Assets

- `PaperCanvas-0.2.0-macOS-arm64.zip` — application with an optimized Rust backend.
- `paper-network-demo.mp4` — 25-second demo with English and Chinese captions (sample papers, illustrative connections, 2× playback).
- `SHA256SUMS.txt` — checksums for the application archive and video.

Dense networks may retain crossing connections. The app notifies you about newer
GitHub releases; updates are downloaded and installed manually.
